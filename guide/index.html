Getting started on the iPad

The iPad edition is a home-screen app: its own icon, works offline, your documents on the device and in your account.

Put Redline on the home screen

  1. 1TapIn Safari on the iPad, open https://abormel15.github.io/redline/.
  2. 2TapTap the Share button (the square with the arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. 3TapOpen Redline from the home screen from now on. It fills the screen and works without a connection.

On a computer open the same address in Chrome or Edge. Chrome also offers “Install Redline” in the address bar.

Sign in so your work follows you

  1. 1TapTap ⋯ More, then Settings, then Redline account › Sign in….
  2. 2TypeEnter your email and password. The first time, tap Create an account; one confirmation email arrives, tap its link, then sign in.
  3. 3LookThe status under the document name says In your account ✓. Sign in the same way on the computer: documents, markups, folders and settings appear there too.

PDFs are kept in the account too, so a document opens on the other device by itself. Settings › Redline account has a switch to keep markups only.

See what changed after an update

  1. 1LookAfter an update a What’s new card opens on its own, with only the newest changes.
  2. 2TapAny time: ⋯ More › What’s new in Redline. All notes lists earlier updates. The build number is shown at the top.

Files

Projects and folders on the Files screen.

Drag documents and folders into folders

  1. 1HoldWith a finger or the Pencil, hold a document card until it lifts. With a mouse, just start dragging.
  2. 2DragDrag it onto a folder card, or onto Projects or a parent folder in the path above the cards.
  3. 3TapSelect several first (the Select button) and they move together.

A quick swipe still scrolls. A folder cannot be dropped into itself. “Move to a folder” in the card’s … menu still works too.

Organize: loose documents into folders in one press

  1. 1TapIn Files, press Organize (next to Sort). It is also the first line under More.
  2. 2LookIt lists the folders it would make for the documents lying loose where you are. By project name puts names that start the same way together (“106 S Lee St survey” and “106 S Lee St bld plan” go into “106 S Lee St”); By month makes one folder per month a document was last changed.
  3. 3TapUntick a folder you do not want, or type a better name for it. Then press Organize. Nothing moves before that.
  4. 4TapChanged your mind? Press Undo on the bar that appears, or More › Undo the last Organize later. Every document goes back and the folders it made are removed.

A document whose name starts with the name of a folder that is already there goes into that folder. One that matches nothing stays where it is. Inside a folder, Organize only looks at that folder.

Bigger page thumbnails

  1. 1DragOpen the Pages panel and drag its right edge. On the iPad the edge is the strip with the small grip mark.
  2. 2LookThe thumbnails grow to fill the panel. Make it wider still and they shift into two, three or more columns.
  3. 3DragReordering works in every layout: drag a thumbnail (on the iPad, hold it for a moment first) and drop it where the bar shows.

The width is remembered. Drag the edge back for a single narrow column.

Crop pages down to what matters

  1. 1DragWith the Rectangle tool, draw a box around the part of the sheet you want to keep.
  2. 2HoldHold on that rectangle and choose Crop this page to the box, or Crop every page to the box for the whole set.
  3. 3LookThe page becomes that area: markups move with it, and a PDF saved while cropped opens cropped for everyone else too.
  4. 4TapChanged your mind? ⋯ More › Undo the crop brings the whole sheet back — nothing was thrown away.

A crop is a window on the page, not a cut, so it can be undone at any time and the file still holds the full sheet.

Bring in a whole folder of drawings

  1. 1TapOn the iPad: in the Files app, hold the folder and choose Compress to make a ZIP.
  2. 2TapIn Redline: New › Bring in a folder (or ⋯ More › Bring in a folder) and pick the ZIP. On a computer it opens a folder picker instead.
  3. 3LookSubfolders become folders, PDFs land in them, a folder of photos becomes one document.

Plan sets

Sheet names, a sheet index and linked callouts.

Sheet names, the sheet index and callouts that jump

  1. 1TapTap ⋯ More › Name the pages from the title blocks. Pages get their sheet number and title (A-2.1 · First floor plan).
  2. 2Tap⋯ More › Sheet index: type a number or a word from the title, tap to jump. Back returns.
  3. 3TapWith the arrow tool, tap a sheet number printed on the page (a detail bubble, “SEE S-1.0”): it is underlined with dots, and the menu offers Go to that sheet.

“Hide sheet links” in that menu turns the dotted underlines off.

A link that lands on an exact view

  1. 1HoldHold on a markup and choose Link to a sheet…, then tap the sheet.
  2. 2DragOn the sheet that appears, drag a box around the part to show, then Use this view. Or choose Whole sheet.
  3. 3TapTapping the blue ↗ on the markup lands zoomed to that box. Back returns to where you were.

Two documents side by side

  1. 1TapOpen the second view (the split button in the top bar) and pick a document for it.
  2. 2TapTap the side you want to edit. It becomes editable where it is — nothing swaps places, your zoom and your tool stay as they were.
  3. 3LookA short “Editing …” note names the document in hand.

Marking up

Construction symbols, looks for the drawing, the keyboard.

Construction symbols

  1. 1TapTap the Symbols tool (Insert group in the left rail). Pick a category or type in the search: outlet, can light, hose bib…
  2. 2TapChoose a Size (S–XL), a Highlight block behind the symbol if you want new work to stand out, and a colour. Tap the symbol.
  3. 3TapTap the sheet wherever it goes; keep tapping for more. Pick another tool when done.
  4. 4HoldA placed symbol: drag a corner to resize, tap a swatch to recolour, hold it for Highlight, Bigger, Smaller.

Electrical, lighting, plumbing, appliances, HVAC, general marks and the drawing marks (section cuts, bubbles, north arrow). They save and flatten like any markup.

Civil and site symbols

  1. 1TapTap the Symbols tool, then the Civil / site group (or type what you want: meter, hydrant, catch basin…).
  2. 2TapMeters, valves and curb stops, backflow preventers, transformers and poles, hydrants, FDC and post indicator valves, fire sprinkler heads, irrigation heads, cleanouts, manholes, catch basins, area drains, downspouts, culverts, swale arrows, spot elevations, benchmarks, bollards, ADA spaces, parking counts, trees and silt fence.
  3. 3HoldA symbol’s letter or number belongs to that markup: double-tap it, or hold it and choose Change the letter or number… — so two water meters can read W1 and W2, and a detail bubble carries this drawing’s number.

Size, colour and the highlight block work the same as the other symbols.

Editing the drawing’s text without bloating the file

  1. 1Tap⋯ More › Make all the text on this page editable now asks first.
  2. 2TapTap the lines I want is the light way: with the Edit text tool, a line becomes a box only when you edit it.
  3. 3TapAlready converted a sheet? ⋯ More › Clean up the text boxes you never changed… (near the bottom of the menu, always there while a document is open) counts the unchanged text boxes, blank covers and empty form boxes — on this page and in the whole document.
  4. 4TapTick what should go, then This page or Whole document. The drawing’s own words show again, and Undo brings the boxes back.

Every cover and box is written into the PDF you save, which is what makes a converted sheet grow — on a test page, seven lines added about 40 KB.

Blueprint look and sharper scans

  1. 1TapTap ⋯ More › How the drawing shows (or hold on the page › Change colors).
  2. 2TapPick Blueprint, Grayscale, Black lines, Faded or Swap a color.
  3. 3TapSharpen scanned pages: Light or Strong makes vendor sheets and scans read crisper on screen.

These change the screen and snapshots only; the saved PDF keeps its original colours.

Looks for the season (and the study)

  1. 1TapTap ⋯ More › Settings › Festive look.
  2. 2TapChoose Automatic (it follows the calendar), a season, or Wizard’s study. Decorations sets animated, still or off.
  3. 3LookColours, the top strip, the home banner and a few icons change. A background or accent you pick yourself always wins.

Mouse, finger and Pencil do the same things

  1. 1HoldRight-click on a computer is holding still on the iPad — on a markup, on one of its handles, or on empty paper, with a finger or the Pencil.
  2. 2TapDouble-click and double-tap both edit a text box, a callout, or a symbol’s letter or number.
  3. 3DragShift-drag (ignore the magnet and guides for one move) is Move it freely once in a markup’s menu. Ctrl-drag (drag away a copy) is Duplicate.
  4. 4TapTab and Shift+Tab between form fields are the ◀ ▶ buttons above the field; Ctrl+P is Save › Print…; Ctrl+wheel is a pinch.

Once the Pencil has been used, fingers move the page and the Pencil works the tools. Settings › Draw with a finger changes that.

Keyboard shortcuts

  1. 1KeyAlt Z fits the selected text boxes to their text.
  2. 2KeyCtrl-drag (⌘-drag on a Mac) a markup to drag away a copy; the original stays.
  3. 3KeyHold Shift while moving or resizing to ignore the magnet, smart guides and grid for that move.
  4. 4KeyCtrl P prints. Tab moves to the next form field, Shift Tab back.
  5. 5KeyCtrl + mouse wheel zooms in steps of about 20% per notch, toward the pointer. The wheel alone scrolls. On the iPad, pinch.

Forms

Fill out any PDF form, fillable or not.

Fill out a form

  1. 1TapOpen the form and tap ⋯ More › Fill out this form. Boxes, underlines, ____ runs and little squares become light-blue fields.
  2. 2TapTap a field to type or tick. Tab or Enter moves to the next one, Shift Tab back.
  3. 3TapFields labelled Name, Address, Phone, Email fill from Settings › My details for forms with one tap; Date fields get today’s date.
  4. 4HoldMissed a box? Hold on the page › Add a text field here or Add a checkbox here. Remove this field for a false hit.
  5. 5TapText too big or too small for the box? While the field is open, the small bar above it has A− and A+, Auto to go back, and All to use that size for every field in the document. Holding on a field gives the same under Text size for this field….

Save PDF turns the fields into real form fields, so the file is fillable in Acrobat and Preview too, with your answers in it. Save flattened PDF prints the answers in. Scanned forms (pictures) are not read yet: add fields by hand there.

Print and save

Getting the work out.

Print

  1. 1TapTap Save › Print… (or Ctrl P).
  2. 2TapChoose This page, All or a Range, and whether the markups are printed on the sheet or left out.
  3. 3LookOn the iPad the PDF opens in the share sheet: choose Print there (or Save to Files). On a computer the print dialog opens straight away; pick paper size and “Fit to page” there.

Save over the file on a computer

  1. 1TapIn Chrome or Edge on a computer, Open uses the system file picker and remembers the file.
  2. 2TapSave › Save PDF then writes over that same file (markups stay editable in it). Save as… picks a new place.
  3. 3LookFlattened PDFs, images and reports always ask where to go. The browser asks permission once per session before the first write.

On the iPad saving goes through Files or the share sheet, as before. Safari and Firefox download instead.

Release notes

Every build, newest first. The same list is under ⋯ More › What’s new in Redline.

Changes by build

86.105 · Nov 24, 2026
  • Pages panel: drag its edge wider and the thumbnails grow to fill it; wider still and they shift into two or more columns. Dragging a thumbnail to reorder works in every layout. On the iPad the edge is now a finger-sized strip with a grip mark.
  • Files › Organize (next to Sort): sorts the loose documents into folders in one press — by project name (names that start the same way go together) or by month. It shows what it will do first, any folder can be unticked or renamed, and Undo puts everything back.
  • Ctrl + mouse wheel now zooms in steps of about 20% per notch. Before, one notch could leap from far out to far in with nothing in between.
86.104 · Nov 24, 2026
  • Forms: what you type now shows in the box as soon as you leave it, and an answer you delete is gone from the page. Before, the page kept showing the box as it was until the document was opened again (the answer itself was always stored and saved correctly).
  • The same goes for checkboxes and pick-from-a-list fields, at any zoom.
86.103 · Nov 19, 2026
  • Nothing changes in how this version works. Under the hood, a copy of the app can now be frozen for sharing: it keeps everything on the device, shows no sign-in, and stays exactly as it is until a newer build is promoted to it on purpose.
